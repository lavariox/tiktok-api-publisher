Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Conecta AI Publisher ("the App") is developed by Conecta US LLC ("we", "us"), 107 Peach Ln, Schertz, TX 78154, USA. This policy explains what data the App collects through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok account identifiers: your TikTok open_id and basic profile information (display name, avatar) obtained via the user.info.basic scope, used only to identify which account authorized the App.
  • Access and refresh tokens: issued by TikTok when you authorize the App, used to upload and publish videos on your behalf.
  • Video files and captions: only the videos and captions you explicitly choose to publish through the App.

We do not collect your TikTok password, private messages, followers lists, or any data beyond what is listed above.

2. How we use your data

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and maintain your authorized session.
  • To upload and publish the videos you review and approve.
  • To refresh access tokens automatically so scheduled posts work.

We never publish content without your explicit approval for each video.

3. Data sharing

Your data is shared only with TikTok as required to operate the App (authentication and publishing). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Data security and retention

Access tokens are stored securely on access-controlled servers and never exposed publicly. Tokens are deleted when you revoke the App's access or request deletion. Published videos remain on TikTok under your account and subject to TikTok's own policies.

5. Your rights

  • You may revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Authorized apps).
  • You may request deletion of your stored data by writing to info@conecthn.com.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent changes.
