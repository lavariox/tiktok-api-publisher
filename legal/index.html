Conecta AI Publisher

Publish your videos to TikTok automatically.

What it does

Conecta AI Publisher helps businesses publish videos to their own TikTok accounts. Log in once with TikTok; scheduled posts go live automatically. Every post is initiated and approved by the account owner — nothing is ever published without explicit approval.

How it works

  • Login Kit — the business owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok's official OAuth consent screen.
  • Content Posting API (Direct Post) — after the owner reviews and approves a video, the app uploads it and publishes it directly to their TikTok account.
  • Secure tokens — access tokens are stored securely and refreshed automatically.
